What is Spice Bag?

Tagline: "Season Every Application Perfectly"

Your CV is like a great recipe. You have all the right ingredients (experience, skills, achievements), but without the right seasoning, it might not stand out.

Spice Bag helps you:

    📊 Analyze what "flavors" each job is looking for
    🧂 Select the right spices from your experience
    ⚖️ Season your CV perfectly for each role
    ✨ Create compelling, tailored cover letters
    📈 Increase your chances of landing interviews

No more generic CVs. Just perfectly tailored applications.
The Metaphor

    Main Ingredient: Your core experience
    Spice Collection: Your skills, achievements, projects
    Recipe: The specific job you're applying for
    Seasoning: Strategic tailoring using AI
    Result: A perfectly flavored application that stands out

📖 Storytelling & Messaging
The Story Behind Spice Bag

"We realized job applications are like cooking:

Your experience is the main ingredient. Valuable. Important. But on its own? Sometimes bland.

Great chefs don't throw away good ingredients—they season them perfectly. They understand that the right spices, in the right amounts, can transform a good dish into something unforgettable.

Your CV deserves the same treatment.

That's why we built Spice Bag. To help you season every application perfectly for every job, so employers can taste exactly how perfect a fit you are."
